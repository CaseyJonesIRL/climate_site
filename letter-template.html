If This Bothers You
A letter template
This is adapted from a real letter a CGA alum sent to his own senator. Fill in the brackets, send it to whoever can actually act — a member of Congress, the Academy Superintendent, the Commandant, or your own alumni association board.
Dear [Recipient], I am a graduate of the U.S. Coast Guard Academy, Class of [Year][, and a (your relevant background — a STEM or public-service career strengthens this)]. I am writing to ask you to press for the rescission of former Secretary Noem's February 14, 2025 directive banning all mention of "climate change" within the Coast Guard and at the Academy. This directive directly undermines Coast Guard readiness. The Academy runs the Center for Arctic Study and Policy — a mission impossible to fulfill while the words "climate change" are formally banned. Every CGA graduate earns a Bachelor of Science; censoring peer-reviewed science from coursework violates that credential. Twenty-five retired senior officers formally asked Superintendent Rothrock to act over a year ago. He has not responded, an outcome many alumni attribute to fear of retaliation. A public statement from you on this issue would matter, and I believe it's well within your reach. Respectfully, [Your name] [Your address, if writing to a government office]
